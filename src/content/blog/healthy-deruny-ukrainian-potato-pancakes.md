Deruny are Ukrainian potato pancakes, crispy, golden, satisfying, traditionally cooked in generous oil until the edges are lace-thin and crunchy. A beloved traditional dish eaten for breakfast, as a starter, or as a light lunch with smetana and chives.
Fried deruny soak up a lot of oil, which is where most of their calories come from. This baked version uses only a light spray of oil: six small pancakes come to about 200 calories, with a crispy outside and the familiar flavour. The secret is in removing the moisture.

## Ingredients
Makes about 30 small deruny (5 servings of 6).

- 800g floury potatoes (Maris Piper, Russet), peeled
- 1 medium onion, finely grated
- 2 eggs
- 100g low-fat cottage cheese (tvaroh), well-drained
- 2 tbsp fresh dill, chopped
- 2 tbsp plain flour OR oat flour (use certified gluten-free oats for a gluten-free version)
- 1/2 tsp salt, pinch of black pepper
- Olive oil spray for the baking tray
- TO SERVE: low-fat sour cream, fresh chives, sauerkraut

## Instructions
- Preheat oven to 220°C / 425°F. Line two large baking trays with parchment. Spray lightly with olive oil.
- Coarsely grate the potatoes and onion into a large bowl.
- Transfer to a clean tea towel. Twist and squeeze firmly, twice, extracting as much liquid as possible. The mixture should be nearly dry.
- Return to a dry bowl. Add eggs, cottage cheese, dill, flour, salt, and pepper. Mix until the batter holds together.
- Scoop 2 tbsp batter per pancake onto trays. Flatten to approximately 1cm thickness with a wet hand.
- Bake on the TOP shelf for 15 minutes until undersides are deep golden.
- Flip carefully and bake 10–12 minutes until second side is equally golden and edges are crisp.
- Serve immediately with low-fat sour cream, fresh herbs, and sauerkraut.

## Nutrition facts
Per serving (6 small deruny, without toppings): about 200 kcal, 8g protein, 3g fat, 34g carbohydrate. A tablespoon of low-fat sour cream adds about 20 kcal. Values are estimates calculated from the ingredients.

## Frequently asked questions

### Why are my baked deruny not crispy?

Four common causes: not enough moisture squeezed out; oven too cool (minimum 220°C); tray overcrowded (steam builds up); pancakes too thick (max 1cm). Fix these and they crisp up well.

### Can I make deruny ahead?

Make the batter up to 2 hours ahead with a squeeze of lemon juice to prevent greying. Cooked deruny reheat well in a hot oven for 5 minutes; a microwave makes them soft.

### What is the difference between deruny and latkes?

They are close relatives: both are grated potato pancakes, deruny from Ukrainian and Belarusian cooking (where they are called draniki), latkes from Ashkenazi Jewish cooking. The basic batter is much the same, potato, onion, egg and a little flour or matzo meal. The cottage cheese in this recipe is not traditional; it adds protein. Both benefit from squeezing out the moisture.
