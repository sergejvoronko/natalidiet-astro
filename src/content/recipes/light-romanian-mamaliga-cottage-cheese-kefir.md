Mămăligă is one of those dishes that feels like a hug from the inside. In Romania, and across much of Eastern Europe, it's more than just polenta; it's a cornerstone of home cooking. It can be a simple side for stews or a main course in its own right, often served with sour cream, cheese, and maybe a fried egg. My mum made this every few weeks, and the simple, earthy smell of cornmeal cooking on the stove is a scent that instantly takes me back to her kitchen. It’s peasant food at its finest—humble, filling, and deeply satisfying.

This version is my own lighter interpretation, designed to be a powerful, protein-packed breakfast that keeps you full for hours. Instead of the traditional heavy sour cream, I use a combination of low-fat cottage cheese and tangy kefir. This swap not only cuts down on fat but also introduces a huge dose of protein and gut-friendly probiotics first thing in the morning. It transforms the dish from a simple carb base into a balanced meal without losing any of its comforting soul. It’s proof that you don’t need to sacrifice flavour or tradition to eat well.

### Serving & Variations

I think this dish is perfect as it is, served hot right out of the pot where the warm mămăligă slightly melts the cheese. The contrast between the creamy, smooth cornmeal and the cool, tangy toppings is just wonderful. If you want to add even more to it, a soft-poached egg on top is fantastic; the runny yolk creates a rich sauce that brings everything together. For a bit of green, some simple sautéed mushrooms or spinach stirred through the mămăligă before serving also works beautifully.

### Storing Leftovers

Mămăligă changes completely once it cools, firming up into a solid block. Don't throw it out! This is my favourite part. You can store the leftover block in an airtight container in the fridge for up to three days. When you're ready to eat, simply slice it into thick pieces and pan-fry them in a little butter or olive oil until golden and crispy on both sides. It's an entirely different experience—a crispy, savoury cake that's amazing with a fresh salad or, you guessed it, another dollop of cottage cheese.
